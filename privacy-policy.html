隱私權政策

一、適用範圍

本隱私權政策適用於「夢想未來記帳本」（以下稱「本 App」），說明本 App 如何處理你在使用夢想規劃、財務盤點、收支日曆、個人設定、台灣雲端發票手機條碼設定及提醒通知等功能時提供的資料。

以本機儲存為核心：目前版本不要求註冊帳號，不提供跨裝置同步，開發者也未營運接收 App 內容的雲端資料庫。

二、處理的資料

依你使用的功能，本 App 可能在你的裝置上處理：

  • 個人設定：使用者名稱及你主動選擇的大頭照。
  • 夢想資料：夢想名稱、圖片、目標金額、累積金額、每月投入金額、預計達成日期及完成時間。
  • 財務資料：個人權益、負債、固定收入、固定支出、交易類型、類別、金額、支付方式、日期、時間及備註。
  • 提醒資料：提醒項目、標題、內容及通知時間。
  • 台灣雲端發票設定：你選擇輸入的財政部雲端發票手機條碼。此欄位為選填，目前只在裝置上保存並檢查格式，不會確認條碼是否已申請。

請勿在備註、名稱或其他自由輸入欄位中填寫不必要的身分證號、金融帳號、密碼或其他高度敏感資訊。

三、資料用途

資料只用於提供你主動使用的功能，包括計算財務總額、依個人權益估算夢想進度、呈現夢想清單、顯示及修改收支紀錄、建立財務日曆、保存個人設定及排定本機通知。

目前版本不將資料用於廣告、使用者輪廓分析、跨 App 或跨網站追蹤，也不販售個人資料。

四、資料儲存與傳輸

你輸入的資料、選取的圖片及設定，原則上儲存在本裝置的 App 本機資料庫。開發者目前不透過自有伺服器接收、保存或同步這些內容。

雲端發票手機條碼目前不會傳送至財政部、開發者或其他第三方。若未來加入財政部或第三方發票服務串接，我們會在啟用前更新本政策及 App 內說明。

若你啟用 iCloud、Google 或裝置製造商提供的備份、移轉或復原服務，資料可能依該服務的設定與政策受到處理；此類處理由平台或裝置服務提供者控制。

五、裝置權限

  • 照片：只有當你主動選擇大頭照或夢想圖片時，App 才開啟 iOS 或 Android 的系統照片選擇器，並保存你選擇的圖片。
  • 通知：只有當你建立提醒並同意系統權限後，App 才依設定時間建立裝置本機通知。

你可隨時在裝置系統設定中調整權限。停用權限可能使對應功能無法使用，但不影響其他本機功能。

六、第三方分享、廣告與追蹤

目前版本不向開發者營運的伺服器上傳你的夢想、財務、個人設定或雲端發票手機條碼，也不向廣告商、資料仲介商或其他第三方出售或提供這些內容。

本 App 不含第三方廣告，也不進行跨 App 或跨網站追蹤。App 商店、作業系統或裝置服務仍可能依其自身政策處理下載、備份、當機或裝置層級的技術資訊。

七、資料保存、控制與刪除

資料保留在你的裝置上，直到你在 App 內刪除資料、解除安裝 App，或使用作業系統提供的清除 App 資料功能。

你可前往「我的 → 系統設定 → 資料 → 刪除本機資料」，刪除夢想、財務、日曆、個人資料、雲端發票手機條碼及提醒設定。刪除後無法復原，App 建立的通知排程也會一併取消。

目前版本不建立使用者帳號，因此沒有開發者伺服器上的帳號或雲端資料需要另外申請刪除。

八、資料安全

本 App 透過裝置本機儲存及作業系統的 App 沙盒機制，降低不必要的網路傳輸與其他 App 直接存取資料的風險。但任何電子儲存方式都無法保證絕對安全，請使用裝置密碼、生物辨識及最新安全更新保護裝置。

九、兒童隱私

本 App 並非專為兒童設計，也不會明知而向兒童蒐集個人資料。若家長或監護人認為兒童在 App 中輸入了不適當的資料，可使用下方聯絡方式與我們聯絡。

十、政策更新

當 App 功能、資料處理方式或適用要求改變時，我們可能更新本政策，並調整「最後更新日期」。如有重大變更，會在 App 內或其他適當位置提供提示。

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to “Aimmora” (the “App”). It explains how the App handles information you provide through dream planning, financial overview, transaction calendar, profile settings, Taiwan e-invoice mobile barcode settings, and reminders.

Local-first: The current version requires no account, offers no cross-device synchronization, and does not use a developer-operated cloud database to receive your in-app content.

2. Information Processed

  • Profile settings: display name and a profile photo you choose.
  • Dream information: title, image, target amount, accumulated amount, monthly allocation, target date, and completion time.
  • Financial information: personal equity, liabilities, recurring income and expenses, transaction type, category, amount, payment method, date, time, and notes.
  • Reminder information: reminder type, title, content, and notification time.
  • Taiwan e-invoice setting: the Ministry of Finance mobile barcode you optionally enter. The current version stores it and validates only its format on your device; it does not verify registration.

Do not enter unnecessary government identification numbers, financial account numbers, passwords, or other highly sensitive information in free-text fields.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide features you choose, including financial calculations, dream-progress estimates, dream lists, transaction records and editing, a financial calendar, profile settings, and local notifications.

The current version does not use this information for advertising, profiling, cross-app or cross-site tracking, or data sales.

4. Storage and Transmission

Information you enter, images you select, and settings are generally stored in the App's local database on your device. The developer does not currently receive, store, or synchronize this content through developer-operated servers.

The Taiwan e-invoice mobile barcode is not currently transmitted to the Ministry of Finance, the developer, or another third party. This policy and the in-app notice will be updated before any government or third-party invoice integration is enabled.

If you use iCloud, Google, or device-manufacturer backup, migration, or recovery services, information may be processed under those providers' settings and policies.

5. Device Permissions

  • Photos: The system photo picker opens only when you choose a profile photo or dream image. The App stores only the image you select.
  • Notifications: The App schedules local notifications only after you create a reminder and grant system permission.

You can change permissions in iOS or Android settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

6. Sharing, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version does not upload dream, financial, profile, or Taiwan e-invoice barcode data to developer-operated servers and does not sell or provide that content to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The App contains no third-party advertising and does not track you across apps or websites. App stores, operating systems, and device services may process download, backup, crash, or device-level technical information under their own policies.

7. Retention, Control, and Deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete it in the App, uninstall the App, or clear App data through the operating system.

Go to “Profile → System Settings → Data → Delete Local Data” to delete dreams, financial and calendar records, profile information, the Taiwan e-invoice barcode, and reminder settings. Deletion cannot be undone and also cancels notification schedules created by the App.

The current version does not create user accounts, so there is no server-side account or cloud data that requires a separate deletion request.

8. Data Security

The App uses on-device storage and the operating system's app sandbox to reduce unnecessary transmission and direct access by other apps. No electronic storage method is completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode, biometrics, and current security updates.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child entered inappropriate information may contact us below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when App features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. We will revise the “Last updated” date and provide an appropriate in-app or other notice for material changes.
